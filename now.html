Bulletin — From the Lake

last filed · 2026 · VII · 25, 09:42 local

Reading

russian short stories

Working slowly through a tattered Penguin anthology — Chekhov in the mornings, Babel after midnight. The point is not to finish; the point is to keep one hand on a second language.

Working on

corrections, not chapters

The defense cleared in April. What is left is the document itself — corrections, formatting, the committee's marginalia in the margins where they belong. Conferral in August. The last mile of a doctorate is, disappointingly, paperwork.

Coding

autoencoder, toward submission

The first version of the protein autoencoder worked but was ugly; the second tries to be honest about its priors. JAX, Flax, a lot of pen-and-paper before any GPU is permitted to start. The manuscript is next.

Off the lake

mid-season

The boat has been in the water since spring and the season is at its loudest. First mate on the paperwork, senior deckhand by Coast Guard designation — both true, neither worth arguing about. The 100-ton master's ticket and the OUPV are in for review; ask me again in the fall.
